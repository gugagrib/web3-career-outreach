# Outreach Lab

These are starting points, not mass-send scripts. Replace every bracket with a real observation about the company, product, or role before sending.

## Founder note

```text
Subject: A growth angle for {{Company}}

Hi {{First name}},

I’ve been exploring {{Company / product}} hands-on and have been particularly interested in {{specific product detail}}.

My focus is growth, user research, community-led acquisition, and product feedback. Through working with Web3 products across exchange, perpetuals, points, and on-chain trading categories, I’ve developed a practical view of how users discover protocols, activate, return, and move through incentive-driven journeys.

I’d be interested in contributing to {{Company}} across {{growth / partnerships / community / product marketing}}. Would you be open to a short conversation about current or upcoming international opportunities?

Best regards,
{{Name}}
{{LinkedIn}} · {{GitHub}} · {{Portfolio}}
```

## Recruiter note

```text
Subject: Web3 growth / product opportunity at {{Company}}

Hi {{Name}},

I’m exploring international opportunities in Web3 growth, partnerships, community, and product marketing. I’ve been actively researching crypto products across exchange, perpetuals, points, and on-chain trading categories.

My strongest value is translating hands-on user experience into structured growth insights, messaging, experiments, and community initiatives. Are there any remote or international roles at {{Company}} that could be a fit?

I’d be glad to share my English CV, GitHub, portfolio, and a short project overview.

Best,
{{Name}}
{{LinkedIn}} · {{GitHub}} · {{Portfolio}}
```

## Personalisation checklist

| Before sending | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| Product detail | One specific observation that could only come from looking at this company. |
| Contribution | One or two relevant ways you could help, not a generic list of skills. |
| Proof | A working GitHub, case study, or portfolio link that can be checked quickly. |
| Ask | A small request for a short conversation or the correct hiring contact. |
| Tone | Direct, respectful, human, and free of airdrop promises or inflated claims. |
